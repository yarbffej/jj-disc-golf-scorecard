Cue #1 · Swing Plane & Footwork +30–45 ft Carry

The "Back-Left to Front-Right" 90° Level Chest Pull

The Symptom: When trying to throw far (e.g., Cal Trail H1 Leopard3 into sand pit, Mize H3/H4 sky-balls), your shoulder dips and launches the disc high with nose-up tilt. At apex, even a -5 turn Mamba or -3 turn Archangel stalls out and dumps hard left.

"Getting my level swing out, pushing the weight to the front right foot as I throw, start with my weight on the back left, not bending that helps, walk through that straight line!" — 10/30 Sar-Ko H6 (Parked Birdie 2)

Actionable Drill: Stand tall without bending at the waist. Start 70% weight on your back left foot, pull the disc across a flat 90° chest line (nipple-to-sternum height), and shift smoothly onto a braced front right leg. On low-ceiling shots (Mize H6 2nd shot), use a pure standstill to guarantee a low release.

Primary Beneficiary Molds: 152g Star Mamba, 169g GStar Roadrunner, 166g GStar Leopard3, 176g Glow Mako3
Cue #2 · Angle & Aim Geometry Hyzer-Flip Mastery

Aiming for Your Natural RHBH Hyzer Tilt by Stability Tier

The Symptom: Your natural comfortable release has a slight right-side-up (RHBH hyzer) tilt. When you throw neutral-to-overstable discs at the basket (e.g., Roc3 on Sar-Ko H2 or Toro on Mize H1/H3), they ride that hyzer tilt and dump 20–35 ft left.

"Almost did a tiny S—right, then fought back left! Farthest was the Rollo, best shot of them all!" — 10/23 Mize 9-Disc Field Test

3-Zone Aim Matrix:
• Understable (-3 to -5 Turn: Rollo, Roadrunner, Mamba): Aim dead straight or 5° left of target—your natural hyzer tilt flips them flat into a dead-straight laser or gentle right-drifting S-curve.
• Neutral (0 Turn: Mako3, Sync, Nova): Aim directly at the pole with zero wrist flick.
• Overstable (+2 to +3 Fade: Toro, Roc3, Eagle, Jay): Aim 15–25 ft right of the pin and let the late left bite work into the bullseye.

Primary Beneficiary Molds: 172g Star Rollo, 174g DX Roc3, 175g Star Toro, 175g Champion Eagle
Cue #3 · Putting & Green Control -2.0 Strokes/Round

"Index Finger Under Rim" + J.Milly Instant Routine + Slope Brake

The Symptom: Multiple rounds show heartbreaking chain-outs/top-band hits (10/28 Mize H1/H4/H5/H8, 10/30 Sar-Ko H2/H9) or 20 ft downhill roll-aways when attacking sloped pins with slick Star plastic.

"Index finger OFF the rim... Just step up and don't even think about the putt!" — 10/22 Sar-Ko H2 & 10/26 Mize H1 J.Milly Key

3. Slope Brake: On downhill/sloped greens (Sar-Ko H9, Harmon H3/H6), switch from slick Star Sync to 175g XT Glow Nova (grippy XT outer rim brakes on contact) or 175g Star Toro.
